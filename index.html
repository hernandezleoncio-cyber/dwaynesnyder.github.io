Qualifications

Dwayne Snyder brings over 35 years of financial leadership and public service experience to the Lowell community. As a former Mayor and long-time City Council member, he understands how local government works and how to make responsible, practical decisions that benefit residents.

His experience, leadership, and commitment to service make him well-qualified to represent the citizens of Lowell with integrity and accountability.
